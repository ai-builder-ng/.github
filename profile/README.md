# AI Builder NG 🚀

### Africa's Practical AI Learning Ecosystem

**Learn • Build • Earn**

AI Builder NG is an AI and technology learning ecosystem designed to help students, professionals, entrepreneurs, creators, and aspiring tech talents develop practical, future-ready digital skills.

We go beyond theory. Learners build real-world projects, use AI-powered tools, participate in practical labs, and develop skills for employment, freelancing, entrepreneurship, and the global digital economy.

🌐 **Website:** https://aibuilderng.live

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make practical Artificial Intelligence and technology education accessible while helping learners transform knowledge into real-world skills, projects, careers, and businesses.

## 🤖 Meet Emma — Our AI Virtual Instructor

**Emma** is the AI-powered virtual instructor inside the AI Builder NG learning ecosystem.
